# Product

## Register

product (the app: lobby, game, settings). The landing page at `#/about` is brand register.

## Platform

web

## Users

- Nostr users who play chess: they have a key already and want something good to do with it. They arrive first and forgive rough edges.
- Lichess players who like free and open: no ads, no tracking, open code, fast boards. They compare every detail.
- Players who want to own their record: a name, games and a rating that survive a ban wave or a shutdown.

The scene: someone at a desk in the evening, or on a phone on the sofa, playing five-minute games for an hour.

## Product Purpose

Clocked chess between Nostr keys. Every move is signed by its player and the finished game is a scoresheet both players sign, so games and ratings are records the players hold, checkable by anyone.

## Positioning

Something a Lichess player adds, not something that replaces Lichess. Thanks to Lichess openly; borrowed rules are credited. Promise: "Play chess under your own key. Both players sign every game, so your games and your rating are yours to keep." Short form: "Chess you sign yourself."

## Conversion & proof

The one action is Play. Proof is the product working: a live or demo board, real checked games from the relay, and the signatures themselves.

## Brand Personality

Plain, exact, a little dry: a good arbiter. Calm, brief, never selling. Chess words before Nostr words ("scoresheet" before "note", "key" before "npub"). Admit limits. The reviewer never accuses.

## Anti-references

- Lichess's warm dark brown and green, Chess.com's green and pawn logo.
- Hype: "revolution", "censorship-resistant future", "Web3".
- Protocol words on screen (event, kind, NIP).

## Design Principles

- The scoresheet is the picture: move list as a ruled sheet, signatures as the proof.
- One accent, ballpoint blue, for actions and everything signed; amber only on zaps; red only on a low clock and losses.
- Detail on demand: each Nostr touch is one line or one icon until asked.
- Restful board, clocks readable at a glance; light and dark both designed.
